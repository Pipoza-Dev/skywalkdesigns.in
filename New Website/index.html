05 — In the details

God is in the details.

A line the studio often returns to. Forged hooks, marble jaali and hammered brass carry a space’s character as much as its plan.

06 — How we work

A clear process, explained at every step.

Listen carefully, show you the design before it’s built, and stay with it until it’s finished.

  1. 01 · Listen & brief

    Your needs, the site, the budget

    We start with your routines, priorities and budget, then visit the site to understand its light, climate and surroundings.

  2. 02 · Plan & visualise

    Plans you can see in 3D

    Space planning, elevations and rendered views, refined with you in detailed consultations until the design feels right.

  3. 03 · Detail & document

    Every surface decided

    Working drawings, material and finish selections, and custom details for joinery, lighting and furniture.

  4. 04 · Build & hand over

    Carried through to the end

    Coordination and site supervision through construction and fit-out, until the space is finished and ready to live in.
