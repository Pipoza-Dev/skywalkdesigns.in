Architecture & planning

Buildings planned around how people live and work: daylight and fresh air in every room, honest materials, and elevations that stay elegant for decades.

  • Custom homes, villas and farmhouses
  • Housing and apartment design
  • Mixed-use and commercial buildings
  • Elevation and façade design
  • Floor plans, space planning and working drawings
  • Landscape, courtyards, gazebos and rooftop decks
  • Home extensions and structural coordination

Interior design

Complete interiors, detailed down to the lighting, joinery and the piece of furniture that makes a room. Calm or celebratory, every scheme starts with how you live.

  • Living and dining rooms
  • Kitchens, from island to modular layouts
  • Bedrooms, wardrobes and dressing rooms
  • Bathrooms, pooja rooms and staircases
  • Lighting design
  • Custom furniture, jaali and joinery details

Retail, workplace & hospitality

Spaces that express a brand and work hard every day: clear circulation, flattering light and materials that stand up to constant use.

  • Fashion boutiques and showrooms
  • Catering, tasting and dining spaces
  • Offices, cabins and meeting rooms
  • Salons and jewellery stores
  • Brand-led interiors and signage zones

Clinics, hospitals & schools

Public buildings need to be clear, durable and calm. We design for patients, students and staff, and for building services that are easy to run.

  • Clinic receptions and waiting areas
  • Hospital façades
  • School auditoriums
  • Automated and dimmable lighting systems

Renovation & remodelling

Older homes often have good bones. We plan renovations that keep what works, fix what doesn’t, and give the whole house a new character.

  • Full-home renovations
  • Kitchen and bathroom remodels
  • Restoration of older buildings
  • New finishes, lighting and services

Questions

Before we begin.

Something else on your mind? Ask the studio

Where do you take on projects?

The studio is based in Jaipur and works across Rajasthan, including Ajmer, Alwar, Bharatpur, Bhilwara, Bikaner, Bundi, Jodhpur, Kota, Sikar, Tonk and more. Get in touch if your site is elsewhere.

Do you only design, or also manage construction?

Both. Appoint us for design only, or for design with project and construction management through to handover. We’ll recommend what suits your project.

Will I see the design before work begins?

Yes. We walk you through plans in detail and prepare 3D views wherever possible, so layout, materials and lighting are decided before anything is built.

Do you take on renovations and single rooms?

Yes. Renovations are a regular part of our work, from complete homes such as the Chitrakoot Residence to individual kitchens, bathrooms and living spaces.

How do we get started?

Send a message on WhatsApp or call the studio. Share what you have, whether that’s a plot, drawings, photos or just an idea, and we’ll arrange a first consultation.
