# Image source log — Skywalk Designs website

Every project photograph on this website was **published by Skywalk Designs itself** on one of its own
channels. Nothing was taken from the previous website, stock libraries, or third-party uploads.

- **Houzz** — the studio's professional profile (`rahul-sharma9931060`):
  https://www.houzz.in/professionals/architects-and-building-designers/skywalk-designs-pfvwin-pf~881802433
- **Instagram** — posts by `@ar.sharmarahul` (Ar. Rahul Sharma), shared as collaborations on `@skywalkdesign`.

Each image is published as WebP at up to four widths: `assets/img/<project>/<name>-<width>.webp` (480, 960, 1600, 2400 px, capped at the original size). Originals are kept in `source/originals/`.
Images were resized and recompressed only. The one exception: `02-farmhouse.jpg` and `08-farmhouse-gazebo.jpg` had an 80 px strip trimmed from the bottom to remove a "REDMI NOTE 5 PRO" phone-camera watermark. Nothing was retouched or altered in content.

**Excluded on purpose:**
- Justdial "service catalogue" photos (generic stock), the low-resolution Justdial renders, and Justdial photos uploaded by users.
- Facebook photos (mostly reposted inspiration images and festival greetings, not Skywalk projects).
- The Instagram photo of two people (April 2023), because the second person couldn't be identified with confidence.

## Brand mark

`assets/brand/logo-mark.svg` is a clean vector redraw of the yellow split-triangle mark the studio uses as its
profile picture on Facebook and Instagram. The colours and geometry match the original. The wordmark is set
in the site typeface, because no official wordmark was found online.

`assets/img/og-image.jpg` (the social share image) is built from the Allure photo `01-dining-jali-arch.jpg`.
