Questions

Before we begin.

Something else on your mind? Ask the studio

Where do you take on projects?{i:plus}

The studio is based in Jaipur and works across Rajasthan, including Ajmer, Alwar, Bharatpur, Bhilwara, Bikaner, Bundi, Jodhpur, Kota, Sikar, Tonk and more. Get in touch if your site is elsewhere.

Do you only design, or also manage construction?{i:plus}

Both. Appoint us for design only, or for design with project and construction management through to handover. We’ll recommend what suits your project.

Will I see the design before work begins?{i:plus}

Yes. We walk you through plans in detail and prepare 3D views wherever possible, so layout, materials and lighting are decided before anything is built.

Do you take on renovations and single rooms?{i:plus}

Yes. Renovations are a regular part of our work, from complete homes such as the Chitrakoot Residence to individual kitchens, bathrooms and living spaces.

How do we get started?{i:plus}

Send a message on WhatsApp or call the studio. Share what you have, whether that’s a plot, drawings, photos or just an idea, and we’ll arrange a first consultation.
