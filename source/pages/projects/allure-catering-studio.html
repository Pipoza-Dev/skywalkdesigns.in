From easy lounges to a tasting room with drama.

Behind a clean white façade, Allure is planned as a sequence of spaces, each with its own mood.

Bright lounges set the tone. White brick walls, patterned armchairs, light timber tables and sculpted brass tree motifs make them relaxed and welcoming, while hexagonal wall panels add touches of colour.

The tasting room

At the heart of the studio is a tasting room in deep teal. Carved jaali arches and windows filter the light, a gold-toned ceiling glows above a long banquet table set with brass, and floral upholstery brings a sense of celebration to every seat.

A navy meeting room with built-in shelving, a playful illustrated mural wall and a dark, jaali-lit passage complete the sequence.
