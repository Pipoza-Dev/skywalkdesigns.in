A modern home that earns its keep.

One of the studio’s first complete residential projects, and the team set out to make an impact. The owner travels often and wanted a modern home with every convenience, on a plot that would also work commercially.

The building rises five floors. The two lower floors are for commercial use, the family lives on the floors above, and a penthouse sits at the top.

Open, bright and naturally ventilated

The home is planned to feel open and generous, with an island kitchen and a double-height living room linked by a floating timber stair. Every space was placed to get plenty of daylight and fresh air.

Outside, the elevation combines concrete and granite textures with white surfaces and timber window frames. Inside, jaali screens, built-in shelving, a mirrored lattice wardrobe and a corner bath carry the same restrained, natural palette.
