A home ready to celebrate.

The house had been bought years earlier and had stood empty ever since. Its owners were moving back for a big occasion: their daughter’s wedding.

To the family the wedding was nothing less than a festival, and they wanted the house to feel just as alive. So the bare structure was given colourful, joyful and eclectic interiors that brought it life and spirit.

Room by room

In the drawing room, floral prints and velvet-feel upholstery make guests feel welcome. A grand pooja room, with wall art and a careful play of light, makes every morning prayer more spiritual. The bedrooms are serene and elegant, keeping the house warm long after the celebrations.

In the end, the house rose to the occasion and celebrated alongside its owners.
