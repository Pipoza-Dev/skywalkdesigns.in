Royal Indian character, reimagined.

An older house needed a face-lift in every part. The clients, a couple, wanted vibrant, dynamic spaces that would bring new life into their home.

The interiors take their cue from rich, royal Indian houses, blending traditional and modern styles.

Gold, texture and craft

Metallic gold textures run through the house, and sofas and cushions in rich fabrics deepen the look. An artistic bar cabinet was designed for the space, and the bathroom fittings are finished in gold to match the theme.

The double-height stair wall is clad in rough sandstone and hung with a cluster of lantern lights, a striking vertical moment at the heart of the house. Both clients were delighted with how their home now looks and feels.
