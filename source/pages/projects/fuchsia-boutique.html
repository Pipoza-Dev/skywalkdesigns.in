A boutique that lets colour lead.

Fuchsia was completed in 2014, the studio’s first year: a fashion boutique where the clothes are the hero.

A warm, neutral backdrop of timber, cream walls and soft chandelier light lets the colourful collections glow. Garments hang from slim rails along the walls, leaving the floor open around a central display counter.

Details with personality

Overhead, a timber-slatted ceiling frames a painted panel in the store’s signature fuchsia with gold stencilled motifs. Carved picture frames in yellow and red add a playful touch to the walls.

At night, the glazed shopfront and illuminated signage turn the boutique into a lantern on the street.
