01 — Our story

Spaces with a sense of character.

Skywalk Designs was founded in 2014 by two IIT Roorkee alumni, Ar. Rahul Sharma and Ar. Shashank Jain, with a straightforward aim: to design and carry out spaces their owners can celebrate.

We believe a space should have a character of its own, drawn from the physical, social and emotional needs of the people who use it. That is why every project starts with listening and a close study of the site.

The studio’s work spans a fashion boutique in its first year, a five-storey mixed-use residence, a catering studio, private homes and renovations, clinic and hospital work and a school auditorium. The approach has stayed the same: understand the people, read the site, resolve every detail.

Today the practice works from its studio on Ajmer Road, Jaipur, and takes on projects across Rajasthan.

02 — Philosophy

Timeless design, in practice.

“Timeless design” is the studio’s founding idea: spaces that never go out of style and still feel young years later.

{i:clock}

Timeless over trendy

Honest materials, good proportion and craft chosen to age gracefully.

{i:compass}

Rooted in Indian planning

Traditional principles of orientation, courtyards, light and air inform every plan.

{i:bulb}

Smart buildings

Today’s technology built in, from automated lighting scenes to services planned for the long term.

{i:users}

Universal design

Spaces that work for everyone who uses them, at every age and every ability.

{i:leaf}

Local & sustainable

Local materials, natural light and ventilation, and a careful eye on running costs.

{i:eye}

Attention to detail

From a forged hook to a marble jaali, the smallest pieces carry a space’s character.
